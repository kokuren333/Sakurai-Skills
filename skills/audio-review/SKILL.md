---
name: audio-review
description: Review game audio hierarchy, feedback, music pacing, ambience, and implementation constraints.
---

# Use when
Audio cues mask one another, lack gameplay clarity, feel mismatched, or audio behavior needs specification.

# Procedure
1. Identify player-critical events, expected emotional role, platform, and mix context.
2. Inspect cue timing, priority, repetition, masking, transitions, ambience, and settings.
3. Tie code findings to behavior cautiously; sound quality requires listening to representative builds.
4. Load relevant `audio`, `gameplay`, and `effects` candidates.
5. Propose listening checks with captions/accessibility and player volume controls considered.

# Output
Listening context, evidence, hierarchy issue, tradeoff, mix/asset/system change, and verification.
