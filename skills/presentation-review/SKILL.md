---
name: presentation-review
description: Review presentation across graphics, motion, effects, sound, and public-facing material.
---

# Use when
Presentation obscures gameplay, lacks impact, conflicts across disciplines, or public material needs review.

# Procedure
1. Identify the gameplay information and emotion presentation should support.
2. Review representative camera, display, audio, and concurrent-event conditions.
3. Check visibility, timing, scale, masking, mix priority, and adjustable settings.
4. For publicity, verify footage and claims against the current product state.
5. Recommend targeted changes and a before/after evaluation.

# Relevant principles
`readability-before-decoration`, `communicate-through-feedback`, `coordinate-audio-visual-priority`, `promote-the-real-product`, `design-for-context`.

# Output
Context, evidence, strengths, presentation risks, tradeoffs, proposed adjustment, verification.

# Guardrails
Do not prescribe stylistic uniformity or make unsupported claims about audience response or source fidelity.
