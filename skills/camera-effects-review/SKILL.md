# Use when
Camera motion, screen shake, effects, hit marks, flashes, or overlapping action signals need review.

# Procedure
1. Identify gameplay-critical subjects and player comfort constraints.
2. Inspect camera framing, motion, effect timing, contrast, occlusion, and concurrent events.
3. Separate style preference from an evidenced visibility or comfort problem.
4. Load relevant `effects`, `graphics`, and `gameplay` candidates.
5. Recommend a representative-scene capture/playtest and respect player-adjustable settings.

# Output
Scene context, evidence, strength, issue, tradeoff, bounded change, and verification.
