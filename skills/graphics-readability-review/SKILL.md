---

# Use when
Players cannot read a scene, target, state, or interface, or art direction needs gameplay review.

# Procedure
1. Identify gameplay information that must remain visible and viewing conditions.
2. Inspect hierarchy, silhouette, contrast, scale, camera distance, background, and competing effects.
3. Distinguish visual taste from a demonstrated readability issue.
4. Load the specific `graphics` candidates relevant to the asset and platform.
5. Propose a comparison capture or representative-build check, including accessibility settings.

# Output
Context, image/build evidence, strengths, readability finding, proposed art change, verification.
