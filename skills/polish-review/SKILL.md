---
name: polish-review
description: Prioritize evidence-backed improvements to a representative game build.
---

# Use when
The core experience exists and the team needs to identify high-value quality improvements.

# Procedure
1. Establish phase, release target, intended players, and build evidence.
2. Gather strengths and issues; distinguish directly observed defects from hypotheses.
3. Prioritize by player impact, reach, confidence, cost, and risk; avoid unsupported severity.
4. Check related gameplay, animation, effects, audio, UI, and performance principles selectively.
5. Define a before/after validation and regression check.

# Output
Ranked findings, evidence, tradeoffs, bounded changes, and verification plan.
