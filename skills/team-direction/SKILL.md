name: team-direction
description: Support creative direction, team communication, decision ownership, and feedback handling.
---

# Use when
Direction is unclear, reviews stall, feedback conflicts, or team roles and decisions need clarification.

# Procedure
1. Identify decision type, accountable owner, contributors, and deadline constraints.
2. Separate creative judgment from facts, specialist risks, and mandatory requirements.
3. Gather relevant input; record the decision rationale and unresolved dissent.
4. Resolve tensions between authorship, consensus, speed, and evidence in context.
5. Load matching `creator_workflow` and `team` principles; do not use them to override accessibility or safety needs.

# Output
Decision statement, evidence/input, owner, tradeoff, next step, and revisit condition.
