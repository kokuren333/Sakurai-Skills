---
name: prototype-review
description: Review an early prototype for learning value, core-loop clarity, and next experiments.
---

# Use when
A playable, mock-up, paper prototype, or technical spike exists and uncertainty remains high.

# Procedure
1. Ask what assumption the prototype is meant to test.
2. Separate representative elements from placeholders and list confounds.
3. Review whether players can understand action and outcome.
4. Compare observations against the hypothesis without treating a small sample as proof.
5. Recommend the next smallest experiment, including what result changes the decision.

# Relevant principles
`rapid-prototyping`, `prototype-before-polish`, `test-with-representative-players`, `communicate-through-feedback`, `design-for-context`.

# Output
Hypothesis, evidence, caveats, learning, next experiment, and a stop/continue/revise recommendation.
