---
name: gameplay-review
description: Review mechanics, player agency, interaction readability, and balance in context.
---

# Use when
Reviewing a mechanic, combat system, movement, progression choice, or gameplay loop.

# Procedure
1. Identify genre, player skill assumptions, intended decisions, and phase.
2. Trace input, response, feedback, consequence, and recovery.
3. Look for dominant strategies, unclear tradeoffs, dead choices, or avoidable interruptions.
4. Check readability under realistic play conditions and concurrent events.
5. Propose a small controlled playtest or parameter experiment.

# Relevant principles
`player-agency-and-tradeoffs`, `communicate-through-feedback`, `readability-before-decoration`, `reduce-unnecessary-friction`, `test-with-representative-players`.

# Output
Mechanic summary, evidence, strengths, issues, tradeoffs, and a testable next adjustment.

# Guardrails
Do not equate difficulty with unfairness or simplicity with accessibility. Judge against the intended audience and evidence.
