---
name: production-review
description: Review scope, specifications, dependencies, team communication, and production risks.
---

# Use when
Planning work, resolving dependencies, writing specifications, reviewing schedule risk, or coordinating change.

# Procedure
1. Identify phase, owners, dependencies, constraints, and decision authority.
2. Check that specifications describe observable behavior and acceptance evidence.
3. Surface blockers, unclear ownership, hidden assumptions, and unnecessary scope.
4. Compare change cost with the cost and player impact of leaving the issue.
5. Make a decision record with owner, next step, and revisit conditions.

# Relevant principles
`specify-for-shared-understanding`, `make-work-visible`, `scope-and-cut-deliberately`, `preserve-core-concept`, `design-for-context`.

# Output
Risks, dependencies, scope decision, owners, validation, and revisit trigger.

# Guardrails
Do not infer team performance from sparse status artifacts. Keep backup, security, compliance, and rollback planning explicit.
