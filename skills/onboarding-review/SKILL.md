name: onboarding-review
description: Review first-use comprehension, tutorial pacing, and learning through play.
---

# Use when
First-session confusion, tutorials, rule teaching, or early-stage player drop-off is in scope.

# Procedure
1. Identify what a new player must understand and what prior knowledge is assumed.
2. Trace the earliest opportunity to act, feedback, safe practice, failure, and optional help.
3. Inspect whether instructions arrive just before they are needed and can be revisited or skipped.
4. Load matching `game_design`, `ui`, and `gameplay` candidates.
5. Propose an observed first-use task with intended players; do not rely only on designer intuition.

# Output
Learning goals, observed friction, evidence gaps, tutorial change, and comprehension test.
