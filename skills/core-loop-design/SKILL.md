---
name: core-loop-design
description: Shape or evaluate the repeated player action, reward, risk, and progression loop.
---

# Use when
Defining a concept, progression loop, reward cadence, or level-to-level motivation.

# Inputs and limits
Use concept/design notes, intended audience, genre, phase, and constraints. Without playable evidence, frame claims as hypotheses.

# Procedure
1. Describe repeated player verbs, decision, immediate consequence, and longer reward.
2. Identify meaningful risks, safe strategies, skill expression, and recovery.
3. Trace where the loop varies, escalates, pauses, or ends.
4. Select candidate principles from semantic clusters `gameplay` and `game_design` that match the specific question.
5. Recommend a small prototype or playtest that can disconfirm the design hypothesis.

# Output
Loop diagram in prose, assumptions, evidence gaps, tradeoffs, proposed change, and validation task.

# Guardrails
Do not impose risk/reward or repetition on experiences whose intended appeal is elsewhere.
