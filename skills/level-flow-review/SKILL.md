---
name: level-flow-review
description: Review navigation, pacing, goals, rewards, and spatial flow through a level or mission.
---

# Use when
Level layouts, missions, quest flow, camera framing, or pacing are under design or review.

# Procedure
1. Identify player goal, critical path, optional routes, and intended duration.
2. Map decisions, landmarks, threats, rewards, transitions, and recovery points.
3. Check whether the next useful action is legible while meaningful exploration remains.
4. Load relevant `game_design`, `gameplay`, and `graphics` candidates from the catalog.
5. Recommend a walkthrough or playtest focused on one pacing/readability question.

# Output
Flow summary, strengths, bottleneck evidence, tradeoffs, change, and validation.
