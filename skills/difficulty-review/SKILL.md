---
name: difficulty-review
description: Review challenge, failure cost, accessibility, and mastery for an intended audience.
---

# Use when
Difficulty spikes, onboarding safety, competitive fairness, or fail/retry loops are being reviewed.

# Procedure
1. Establish intended audience, mastery curve, stakes, and challenge fantasy.
2. Trace challenge signal, player options, failure cause, recovery, and retry cost.
3. Separate intended challenge from opaque rules, control ambiguity, and avoidable barriers.
4. Select relevant `gameplay` and `game_design` candidates; compare countervailing principles.
5. Recommend a bounded test across appropriate skill levels and access needs.

# Output
Challenge profile, evidence, likely cause, audience-specific tradeoff, adjustment, test.

# Guardrails
Do not equate lower difficulty with accessibility or higher difficulty with quality.
