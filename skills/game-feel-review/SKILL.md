---
name: game-feel-review
description: Review controls, timing, action response, hit feedback, and sensory coordination.
---

# Use when
Controls feel unresponsive, actions lack weight, feedback is unclear, or sensory signals compete.

# Procedure
1. Identify intended action timing and player expectation.
2. Trace input acceptance, startup, active response, recovery, and feedback.
3. Inspect timing, location, magnitude, consistency, and cancellation/retry behavior.
4. Review visual/audio signals for masking, accessibility settings, and sensory intensity.
5. Recommend a bounded tuning experiment and comparison capture.

# Relevant principles
`communicate-through-feedback`, `readability-before-decoration`, `coordinate-audio-visual-priority`, `player-first`, `design-for-context`.

# Output
Action trace, observed mismatch, evidence, tradeoff, adjustment, and validation plan.

# Guardrails
Do not invent frame or decibel targets without project evidence. Respect user-adjustable accessibility options.
