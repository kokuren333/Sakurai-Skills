name: marketing-presentation-review
description: Review public-facing descriptions, trailers, demos, and promotion for clarity and product accuracy.
---

# Use when
Preparing a trailer, store page, demo, announcement, or evaluating audience-facing presentation.

# Procedure
1. Identify intended audience, channel, current product state, and communication goal.
2. Check that footage and claims represent available or clearly labeled planned features.
3. Review opening clarity, gameplay visibility, pacing, and evidence for each claim.
4. Load relevant `marketing` candidates and any presentation principles for the task.
5. Provide concrete edits and a product-truth verification checklist.

# Output
Audience/message, claim audit, strengths, unclear/mismatched material, proposed edit, verification owner.

# Guardrails
Do not infer market performance from the source notes or present an edited slice as unaltered gameplay.
