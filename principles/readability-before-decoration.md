# Protect gameplay readability

## Summary
Prioritize information needed for decisions when balancing effects, animation, audio, UI, and scene composition.

## SOURCE
Presentation materials connect visual design to legibility and visibility. This is a thematic attribution to supplied intermediate material, not a verified quotation or timestamp. See [preserved source](../sources/raw/F-graphics.md).

## GENERALIZED
Inspect camera distances, backgrounds, motion, and concurrent effects.

## AGENT
Needs vary by genre, audience, display size, and deliberate ambiguity.

## Apply when
- Project scope includes this decision and context supports the intended outcome.

## Questions
- What player-visible outcome is at stake?
- What evidence supports this diagnosis?
- Which constraints or countervailing principles matter?

## Review criteria
- Tie advice to evidence or explicit assumptions.
- State tradeoffs, exceptions, and a small validation step.
