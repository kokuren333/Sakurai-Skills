# Make progress and problems visible early

## Summary
Use regular lightweight artifacts and direct feedback so dependencies and risks surface in time.

## SOURCE
Team materials emphasize visual progress sharing and early expression of concerns. This is a thematic attribution to supplied intermediate material, not a verified quotation or timestamp. See [preserved source](../sources/raw/E-team-management.md).

## GENERALIZED
Choose an artifact and cadence appropriate to team size; assign owners and next steps.

## AGENT
Avoid rituals whose cost exceeds the information they provide.

## Apply when
- Project scope includes this decision and context supports the intended outcome.

## Questions
- What player-visible outcome is at stake?
- What evidence supports this diagnosis?
- Which constraints or countervailing principles matter?

## Review criteria
- Tie advice to evidence or explicit assumptions.
- State tradeoffs, exceptions, and a small validation step.
