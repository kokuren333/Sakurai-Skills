# Make meaningful choices carry legible tradeoffs

## Summary
Give players decisions whose alternatives have understandable advantages and costs.

## SOURCE
Gameplay material asks whether strong options contain weaknesses and players have active choices. This is a thematic attribution to supplied intermediate material, not a verified quotation or timestamp. See [preserved source](../sources/raw/B-game-essence.md).

## GENERALIZED
Review dominant strategies, risk/reward, and whether choices change situations.

## AGENT
Some experiences intentionally emphasize mastery, expression, or flow over explicit choice.

## Apply when
- Project scope includes this decision and context supports the intended outcome.

## Questions
- What player-visible outcome is at stake?
- What evidence supports this diagnosis?
- Which constraints or countervailing principles matter?

## Review criteria
- Tie advice to evidence or explicit assumptions.
- State tradeoffs, exceptions, and a small validation step.
