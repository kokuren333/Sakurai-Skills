# Manage scope deliberately

## Summary
Compare value, cost, dependencies, and risk; cut or defer low-value scope when it protects the central experience.

## SOURCE
Team material discusses cutting waste and correcting direction. This is a thematic attribution to supplied intermediate material, not a verified quotation or timestamp. See [preserved source](../sources/raw/E-team-management.md).

## GENERALIZED
Make decisions explicit and record what evidence would reopen them.

## AGENT
Assess accessibility, safety, compliance, and core content separately.

## Apply when
- Project scope includes this decision and context supports the intended outcome.

## Questions
- What player-visible outcome is at stake?
- What evidence supports this diagnosis?
- Which constraints or countervailing principles matter?

## Review criteria
- Tie advice to evidence or explicit assumptions.
- State tradeoffs, exceptions, and a small validation step.
