# Use evidence from intended players

## Summary
Gather observations from representative players and separate observed behavior from interpretation.

## SOURCE
The source corpus emphasizes objective viewpoints and player experience. This is a thematic attribution to supplied intermediate material, not a verified quotation or timestamp. See [preserved source](../sources/raw/C-planning-design.md).

## GENERALIZED
Record context, task, observed behavior, interpretation, and uncertainty.

## AGENT
Small or expert-only samples cannot establish broad audience behavior.

## Apply when
- Project scope includes this decision and context supports the intended outcome.

## Questions
- What player-visible outcome is at stake?
- What evidence supports this diagnosis?
- Which constraints or countervailing principles matter?

## Review criteria
- Tie advice to evidence or explicit assumptions.
- State tradeoffs, exceptions, and a small validation step.
