# Keep development constraints out of the experience

## Summary
Where feasible, solve production limitations in implementation rather than transferring avoidable friction to players.

## SOURCE
The source material discusses protecting the player perspective from development-side convenience. This is a thematic attribution to supplied intermediate material, not a verified quotation or timestamp. See [preserved source](../sources/raw/A-work-attitude.md).

## GENERALIZED
Translate internal constraints into clear, low-friction player-facing behavior.

## AGENT
Some constraints must be communicated honestly, including network, accessibility, safety, and platform limits.

## Apply when
- Project scope includes this decision and context supports the intended outcome.

## Questions
- What player-visible outcome is at stake?
- What evidence supports this diagnosis?
- Which constraints or countervailing principles matter?

## Review criteria
- Tie advice to evidence or explicit assumptions.
- State tradeoffs, exceptions, and a small validation step.
