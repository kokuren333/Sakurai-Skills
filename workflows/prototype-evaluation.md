# Prototype evaluation
1. State the design hypothesis, target player, prototype limitations, and success/disconfirming observations.
2. Confirm the build or model represents the interaction being tested.
3. Give players a concrete task without coaching unless coaching is part of the intended experience.
4. Record action sequence, hesitation, errors, recovery, and player comments separately.
5. Identify confounds and avoid generalizing beyond the sample.
6. Choose continue, revise, or stop with one next experiment and owner.
