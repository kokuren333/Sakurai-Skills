# Domain prompts

## UI
- Can a first-time player infer focus, available actions, and consequences?
- Are repeated actions, skipping, pausing, remapping, and text pace appropriate?

## Tutorial
- What is taught, when, and through which interaction or signal?
- Can players retry or consult information without undue interruption?

## Combat
- Are attack intent, active area, hit location, result, and recovery readable?
- Do strong choices expose understandable tradeoffs for this genre?

## Multiplayer
- Are state changes legible to all relevant players?
- Does camera, audio, or visual prioritization hide important information?
- Have network and competitive constraints been reviewed as product requirements?
