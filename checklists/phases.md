# Phase questions

## Concept
- Who is the intended player, and what do they repeatedly do?
- What is the central promise and how can a small prototype test it?
- What makes this experience distinct?

## Prototype
- What risky assumption is currently tested?
- Can players understand cause and effect with placeholder assets?
- What did representative players do, separately from what they said?

## Vertical slice
- Are controls, feedback, UI, audio, and visual readability coherent together?
- Which production assumptions remain unproven at target quality?
- Are accessibility and performance risks visible?

## Production
- Are behavior, ownership, dependencies, and acceptance evidence clear?
- Is new scope strengthening the core promise enough to justify its cost?
- Are progress and problems visible to people who can act on them?

## Polish
- Which player-visible issue has greatest impact and strongest evidence?
- Does the change improve readability and feel without masking other signals?
- Can the result be compared in a before/after review?

## Release
- Do public claims, footage, and availability match the shipped state?
- Are known issues, settings, and support paths clear?
- Have rollback, backup, platform, and accessibility readiness been reviewed?
